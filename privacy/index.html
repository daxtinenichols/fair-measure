Privacy policy

Your records stay with you

Last updated 10 October 2026.

The short version: the tools run in your browser and store your business records on your device. We don't receive them, so we can't read, lose, sell or share them.

What stays on your device

Your business details, logo, customers, items, quotes, invoices and payments are saved in your web browser's local storage on the device you're using. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you download a backup, that file is saved wherever you choose.

Shift Planner keeps your team's names, contact details, pay rates and schedules the same way, on your device. When you share a week, the schedule (names, roles and shift times only, never phone numbers or pay) is packed into the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to any server. Whoever you give the link to can read that week's schedule.

Because the records live only on your device, clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or losing the device can erase them. Please keep backups.

What we receive

  • Visiting the site. Our pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Buying Pro. Checkout is run by our payment partner, which acts as the seller of record. They collect your name, email, payment and billing details to process the sale, handle tax and send your license key. We receive your name, email, country and order details so we can support you and issue refunds. We never see your full card number.
  • Checking a license key. When you enter a Pro key, your browser sends the key to our payment partner to confirm it, and re-checks it about once a month. No business records are sent.
  • Writing to us. If you email us at fairmeasuresupport@gmail.com, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

What we don't do

We use no cookies, no advertising, no analytics trackers and no third-party scripts in our tools. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for anything beyond running Fair Measure.

Your choices and rights

You can view, change, export or erase your business records at any time from My business. For information we hold about a purchase, write to us at fairmeasuresupport@gmail.com and we'll send it, correct it, or delete it where the law allows (we must keep some sales records for tax purposes). Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have further rights, and you can exercise them the same way.

Children

Fair Measure is made for businesses and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we'll say so on the home page.
