Refund policy

30 days, no questions

Last updated 10 October 2026.

If Fair Measure Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of buying it and you'll get every cent back.

How to ask

Write to fairmeasuresupport@gmail.com, or reply to your receipt email. Tell us the email you bought with. You don't have to give a reason, though we're always glad to hear how we could do better.

What happens next

  • We approve the refund, usually within two business days.
  • Our payment partner returns the money to your original payment method. Banks typically take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • Your license key is switched off. Your records stay on your device, and the free tools keep working for you.

After 30 days

If something has gone wrong after 30 days (for example, a feature you paid for stopped working), write to us anyway at fairmeasuresupport@gmail.com. We'll make it right.
