Good questions

Where is my data kept?

In your web browser, on the device you're using. Nothing is sent to us. That's why there's no account. To move to another device, or to keep a safe copy, download a backup file from My business and open it on the other device.

What if I clear my browser or lose my phone?

Your data goes with it, the same as a paper notebook. Download a backup now and then; it takes one click and the file is small. We'll gently remind you.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the page in any modern browser. You can add it to your home screen if you like, and it keeps working without internet once loaded.

Who handles payment and sales tax on my purchase?

Pro is sold through a trusted payment partner that acts as the seller of record. They take payment, handle sales tax, and email your license key. We never see your card.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. Our tools do the paperwork and the arithmetic carefully, but you know your business and your local rules. When in doubt, ask an accountant.

Why "Fair Measure"?

From Proverbs: "a just weight is his delight." We try to give honest value at an honest price, and to help you do the same for your customers. More about us.
