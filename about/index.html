About

Honest tools, at an honest price

Fair Measure is a small, independent workshop making software for the people who keep main street running: carpenters and cleaners, bakers and bookkeepers, florists, mechanics and makers.

Why we make these

Most business software is now rented by the month, priced per person, and paid for partly with your data. For a one-person business, those fees add up, and the tools are often more complicated than the job.

We think a small business deserves tools that are simple, sturdy, and yours. So we build them to run in your browser, keep your records on your own device, and sell them for one fair price.

What we promise

  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics that follow you, no selling or sharing data. We don't have your data to sell.
  • No tricks. No fake countdowns, no buried cancellation, no surprise renewals. There's nothing to cancel.
  • A fair deal. If Pro doesn't serve you, ask for a refund within 30 days and you'll get it.
  • Careful work. We test the arithmetic, keep the pages fast and accessible, and fix what's broken.

The name

"Fair Measure" comes from an old idea, found in Proverbs 11:1: "A false balance is an abomination to the Lord, but a just weight is his delight." We're a Catholic-run business, and we try to let that show in how we work: honest dealing, a just price, respect for the people we serve and for their work.
